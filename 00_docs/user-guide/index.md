# User Guide

Welcome to the Sākṣī User Guide. This section will help you install, configure, and use the Sākṣī applications on your Android device.

## What is Sākṣī?

Sākṣī helps you preserve important photos and videos by saving a separate, synchronized copy in a private vault. If a photo is deleted from your normal Gallery, the copy in Sākṣī Vault is not affected.

For a detailed explanation of how it works and what it protects against, read our [Security Overview](../security-privacy/README.md) and [Privacy Policy](../security-privacy/privacy-policy.md).

## Getting Started

Because Sākṣī is a modular system, you need to install at least the Vault and one client application (like Camera or Portal).

*   **[Installation Guide](getting-started/installation.md):** Step-by-step instructions for installing the necessary Sākṣī applications.

## Using Sākṣī Camera

Sākṣī Camera looks and acts like a normal camera app, but it is designed to securely synchronize your recordings to the Vault, even if the recording is interrupted.

*   **[Recording & Sync](camera/recording-and-sync.md):** Learn about crash-safe fMP4 recording and how your media is saved.
*   **[Changing the Vault](camera/changing-vault.md):** How to manage connection settings.

## Managing Media with Sākṣī Portal

Sākṣī Portal is the app you use to browse, view, and export the media stored in your Vault.

*   **[Browsing & Exporting](portal/browsing-and-exporting.md):** How to view your saved files and export them back to your public gallery.
*   **[Scheduled Deletions](portal/scheduled-deletion.md):** Learn about the soft-delete grace period and how to recover files.
*   **[App Lock Security](portal/app-lock.md):** How to protect the Portal interface with your device PIN or fingerprint.
