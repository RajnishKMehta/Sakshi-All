# Browsing and Exporting Media

The Sākṣī Portal app is your gateway to viewing the files stored securely inside the Vault. It operates entirely offline.

## Viewing Media

When you open the Portal, you will see a grid of the media currently stored in the connected Vault.

* Tap any thumbnail to open the full-screen viewer.
* Video files are marked with a Play icon in the center of the thumbnail.

## Exporting (Saving) to Your Device

Because files in the Vault are stored separately from your normal Android gallery, you must export them if you want to share them or edit them with other apps.

1. Tap a photo or video in the Portal gallery to open the viewer.
2. Tap the **Save to device** (download) icon in the top right corner.
3. The Portal will process the file and save a copy to your phone's standard download/media storage.
4. A "Saved to..." message will appear confirming the location.

*Note: Exporting creates a normal, visible copy on your device. The original file remains securely inside the Vault.*
