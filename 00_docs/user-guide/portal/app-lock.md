# App Lock

To protect the media you view through Sākṣī Portal, you can enable App Lock. This requires you to authenticate (using your device's PIN, pattern, or biometrics) before you can access the app.

## Prerequisites

Your Android device must already have a secure lock screen configured (PIN, Password, Pattern, or Fingerprint). If your device does not have a screen lock, you cannot enable App Lock in Portal.

## Enabling App Lock

1. Open the **Sākṣī Portal** app.
2. Tap the **More Options** (three vertical dots) menu.
3. Tap **Settings**.
4. Scroll down to the **Security** section.
5. Toggle the **App Lock** switch to enable it.
6. If your device supports it and is configured correctly, App Lock is now active.

## Configuring the Timeout

By default, Portal locks after 1 minute when you leave the app. You can change the timeout before Portal requires you to authenticate again.

1. In the Portal **Settings**, under the **Security** section (with App Lock enabled), tap **Automatically lock after**.
2. Select your preferred timeout duration:
   - Immediately
   - 30 seconds
   - 1 minute
   - 5 minutes
   - 10 minutes

If you leave the app and return before this time expires, you will not need to authenticate again. If you exceed the time, the lock screen will appear.
