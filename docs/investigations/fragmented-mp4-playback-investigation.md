# Fragmented MP4 Playback Investigation

## Investigation Scope
- **Date**: 2026-10-23
- **Objective**: Investigate and diagnose why fragmented MP4 files generated by the `02_vault` / `03_camera` modules fail to play audio and have seeking issues in most players (including ExoPlayer/Media3), and implement the correct fix based on evidence.

## Executive Summary
An evidence-based investigation confirmed that the custom `FragmentedMedia3Muxer` (in `:03_camera`) was missing essential AAC codec configuration data (`mp4a.40.2`) and omitted the required `BUFFER_FLAG_KEY_FRAME` flag for audio samples. Due to a known issue in older versions of Media3 (`<1.8`)—the version in this repository is `1.11.1`, but still requires proper syncing flags for independent audio frames—the generated fragmented MP4 files contained incorrectly flagged AAC audio samples in `trun` boxes (marked as non-sync, `Sync="0"`). This prevented ExoPlayer and other platform players from properly extracting the audio and resolving seeking timelines correctly. The issue was fixed by injecting `.setCodecs("mp4a.40.2")` into the audio track format and forcing `BUFFER_FLAG_KEY_FRAME` for all audio samples.

## Reported Symptoms
- VLC could play the video, but ExoPlayer (and the internal `:04_portal` player) could not.
- Seeking forward or backward failed.
- Audio was completely inaudible.

## Current Documentation & Research
1. Media3 Issue [#2435](https://github.com/androidx/media/issues/2435): Identified that `FragmentedMp4Muxer` incorrectly flags AAC samples as non-sync in `trun` boxes unless `BUFFER_FLAG_KEY_FRAME` is explicitly provided or the codec explicitly overrides it.
2. The recommended workaround for explicit AAC processing was to add `.setCodecs("mp4a.40.2")` to the `Format.Builder` and ensure that all audio samples carry `C.BUFFER_FLAG_KEY_FRAME`.

## Findings
### `03_camera/app/src/main/java/rajnishkmehta/sakshi/camera/capturer/FragmentedMedia3Muxer.kt`
- **Audio Codec Data**: The builder missed `.setCodecs("mp4a.40.2")` when processing `MIMETYPE_AUDIO_AAC`.
- **Keyframe Flags**: Only buffers marked by MediaCodec with `BUFFER_FLAG_KEY_FRAME` were translated to `C.BUFFER_FLAG_KEY_FRAME`. Since AAC encoders rarely mark every frame as a sync frame in the `BufferInfo` flags (despite AAC frames being independently decodable), the Media3 muxer recorded them as dependent frames, corrupting seeking and playback.

### `04_portal/` Playback
- Playback issues were a direct consequence of the malformed fMP4 generated by `03_camera`, specifically the `trun` syncing errors. No changes were required in the player itself.

## Root Cause
The root cause was a combination of Media3's strict `trun` sync expectations and the absence of `BUFFER_FLAG_KEY_FRAME` explicitly applied to independently decodable audio frames (AAC).

## Fix Implemented
1. Modified `FragmentedMedia3Muxer.kt` to maintain a track-to-MIME-type mapping (`mimeTypeMap`).
2. Added `.setCodecs("mp4a.40.2")` when `mimeType` is `MIMETYPE_AUDIO_AAC`.
3. In `writeSampleData()`, forced `media3Flags` to include `C.BUFFER_FLAG_KEY_FRAME` if the track corresponds to a supported audio MIME type.
